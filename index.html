Our story

From one mop to a movement

Reali Clean started as a one-woman operation with a simple mission: bring comfort and calm into people’s homes through reliable, detail-driven cleaning. As a solo cleaner juggling family life and a growing business, every spotless room was a reflection of pride, care and resilience.

Word spread, and so did we. Today our trained team serves homes, offices, vacation rentals and commercial spaces across Northern New York, and we treat every property with the same care we give our own.

We know how busy life gets. Between work, kids and everything in between, cleaning falls to the bottom of the list. That’s where we come in.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

Something else on your mind? Call or text (315) 804-4188 and we’ll help.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every job is priced individually based on your space and what you need, and quotes are always free. Reach out by phone, email, Facebook, or the form below and we’ll get back to you with details.

Can I book just a one-time cleaning?

Absolutely. One-time deep cleans and move-in/move-out cleans are some of our most popular services. Fair warning though: we think you’ll love it so much you’ll be calling us back.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?

Nope. We bring safe, effective, professional-grade products and all the equipment we need. If you have a preferred product for a particular surface, just let us know.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and debit or credit cards.

What is your cancellation policy?

Life happens. We just ask for 24 to 48 hours’ notice for any appointment cancellations so we can adjust our schedule.

Do you clean on Fort Drum?

Yes. We serve on-base housing and the surrounding area, and we’re happy to help with move-out cleans when it’s time to PCS.

Do you offer carpet shampooing on its own?

Yes. Carpet shampooing and hard floor strip-and-wax are available as standalone services or as an add-on to any clean, for both homes and businesses.
